Input two turns, pinion quarter turn and magazine six-degree step complete together at frame 455, then hold. All mounted records, paper, pins, shoes and groove bands share that indexed transform; the fixed drive casing stays still. A brief matched foreground metal housing wipe across frames 398–411 conceals the existing difference in scale between the approved hall builder and the record transfer builder; the hall-to-transfer cut is documented rather than represented as one literal scene. The close camera rises above adjacent record rims to see a printed label, then cranes over the complete radial array. The active record uses the original UV-mapped artwork and geometry. The same printed magazine presentation continues into the vinyl scene; its paper gradually converges to the original source label colours across frames 840–915, then exact original label materials and geometry return at C1. Narrow lighting and concealed fluorescent tube geometry keep the camera clear during the crane. The new 24-tooth pinion has a nominal pitch radius of 44.7 against the internal ring’s 670.5, at centre radius 625.8; the teeth have .15 clearance. Their rotations have the same sign and matching pitch-surface velocity. The original undersized pinion is hidden only in this new scene.

## Continuous microscopic journey

The entire approach, descent, curved groove flight, emergence and stylus shot use the original record-space coordinate system, original arm and environment. The flight sector is parented to the same rotating record. Pitch .4, valley depth .13 and groove width .26 retain the approved physical ratio; a rounded cross-section and original deterministic spiral/audio lateral modulation give smooth pressed vinyl. Travel is about 2.75 scene units over 5.27 seconds, so five recessed service stations, one inspection gantry and a low inspection carriage remain readable. Wall hardware has cooling ribs, brass hex fasteners, conduits, handrails and restrained amber window lighting.

The original full-disc solid base has its upper surface at z=-.004, which would fill a real .13-deep groove. It is therefore culled while the camera is near/below the surface (frames 639–849), then fades into the distant skin through frame 868; the smooth local sector closes the visible surface. During the escape across frames 813–867, a shape key smoothly converges the local visible depth to the original solid base skin at -.0039. Surface and specular normals then crossfade across frames 850–868 from the smooth flight sector to the original base and patch, preventing the original hidden-ridge normals from popping. Original materials are restored at frame 868. The true .13 depth is retained throughout the submerged flight. This explicit geometry LOD prevents the otherwise visible one-frame valley-fill pop at a three-unit camera height; stations become occluded under the closing surface. Original geometry is fully restored before the exact C1 join. The journey remains on one record.

The final flight mesh uses about 135,225 vertices over a 22-unit sector around the 2.75-unit path. The earlier 93-unit sector and distant magazine groove-band meshes were unnecessary at this scale; omitting those subpixel details reduces geometry while retaining the actual discs and printed-paper presentation. A repeat full-quality micro benchmark was 120.72 seconds against 121.10 before the geometry reduction; this is a memory/geometry improvement, not a demonstrated render-speed gain.

The micro camera has clip start .00001, stays on the actual modulated channel centre, and sits around z=-.075 with at least .051 clearance above the valley floor. The original C1 camera retains its original .02 clip start. No floating-origin or fake enlarged world is necessary at this scale.

All 135 integer samples from global frames 915 through 1049 are compared against the original builder's corresponding source poses before time remapping. Position, orientation, lens and record angle must agree. The inverse time mapping is strictly monotonic, including source frames 239→947, 240→948, 440→1048 and 443→1049. `--validate-only` evaluates all native frame poses plus quarter-frame micro-camera samples without rendering pixels, writing `c1-source-equivalence.json`, `micro-subframe-clearance.json` and `render-vinyl.json`. This is a geometry/camera check; visual transition review is also required.

## Renderer controls

Direct CLI output is protected by per-shot source/asset/settings fingerprints and CRC/dimension PNG checks. Images write to a hidden temporary PNG and move atomically into place. Native entry plates supplied by the production runner are retained via environment defaults. The default full-resolution guard blocks long frame ranges; the explicit `--render-full`/`--production-render` option enables the future authorized production run. It has not been invoked during development. GPU work must run serially with Chrome compositor acceptance tests and other Blender processes.

Saved spot `.blend` files capture the selected inspection state. The Python scene builders and CLI perform the camera/visibility setup for each global frame and are the authoritative animation render path.
